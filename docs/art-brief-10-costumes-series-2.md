## Part 3 — Series 2 concept board

Series 1 began with an approved concept board before any pose was drawn. Series 2 should start the same way: one board, approve or change each character, then a full pose brief (13 files per character, as in Series 1).

### Proposed theme: "Teatime Friends" and a new Kitchen room

Small bakery-and-teatime plush friends who move in when the cottage gets a Kitchen. It gives Series 2 its own look (warm creams, caramels, berry reds) next to Series 1's night-and-garden mix, and it fits the cottage without needing a whole new location. Same rarity mix as Series 1.

| # | Name | Rarity | One-line idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crumb | Common | A biscuit-brown mouse baker with flour on its nose, always nibbling the edges |
| 2 | Mallow | Common | A squishy cream hamster who puffs up round when happy |
| 3 | Pekoe | Common | A calm tea-loving otter who wears a teacup lid as a hat |
| 4 | Jammy | Common | A strawberry-red hedgehog with sticky paws and a jam-jar-lid shield |
| 5 | Whisk | Common | A fluffy yellow duckling who stirs everything far too fast |
| 6 | Dumple | Common | A round, sleepy steamed-bun panda cub who naps in the bamboo steamer |
| 7 | Sprinkle | Rare | A pastel kitten speckled with confetti dots, who decorates whatever sits still |
| 8 | Toffee | Rare | A caramel-striped raccoon who sneaks midnight snacks and leaves thank-you notes |
| 9 | Soufflé | Epic | An elegant cream swan pastry chef who only "rises" when the room is perfectly calm |
| 10 | Cherri | Secret | A tiny cherry-red sprite who appears on top only when the whole table is set |

Other themes considered: **Seaside** (shells, lighthouse, a beach hut area) and **Attic** (forgotten toys, music box, dress-up trunk). Either can replace Teatime; the prompt below only needs the theme line and table swapped.
