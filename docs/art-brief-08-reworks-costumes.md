## Part 1 — Pippo: replace the goggles (13 files)

Pippo's aviator goggles look too much like Tinker's signature goggles. Keep everything else about Pippo (apricot-orange body, brown accents, big fluffy tail, cheerful traveller personality) and replace the goggles with:

- a soft knitted travel beanie with a little felt paper-aeroplane badge, and
- a small rolled-up map tucked into a shoulder strap.

No goggles, lenses or pilot gear anywhere. The Decorated set keeps the postal cap and stamp satchel (no goggles there either).

Files: `char_pippo_master`, `_idle`, `_walk`, `_walk2`, `_sit`, `_sleep`, `_happy`, `_portrait`, `_decorated_idle`, `_decorated_walk`, `_decorated_walk2`, `_decorated_sit`, `_decorated_sleep` (each as `_v2`, or `_v3` for decorated_sleep).

## Part 2 — Glim: periwinkle blue (13 files)

Glim and Mopey are both lavender with large purple hoods and look too alike. Rework Glim to match the original brief: **periwinkle blue body with a soft yellow glow** (a small glowing star or lantern-light on the tail tip or antenna). Replace the large purple hood with no hood at all; Glim's silhouette should read as a small round night-light creature. The Decorated set keeps the crescent-moon clip and starry knitted shawl, recoloured to suit blue.

Files: the same 13 names as Part 1 with `char_glim_` (each as `_v2`, or `_v3` for sleep).
